### Added

- **The commit follows a logical node or a logical device moved within its IED.** An `LN` moved with `tx.move` to another `LDevice` of its IED, or an `LDevice` moved to another `AccessPoint` of its IED, now commits: every tuple reference naming what moved by its uuid (`LNode`, `ExtRef`, `FCDA`, `ClientLN`, `IEDName`, `Association`, `ExtCtrl`) takes its names at the new place, from the document the move step reads once; a reference naming the new place by names only takes the uuid of what now stands there, as after a rename; a `LogControl` with no `ldInst` in a moved logical node gets the `inst` of the device it was in; the `GSE` / `SMV` of a moved device's control blocks go to the new access point's `ConnectedAP` on the same subnetwork, address kept. A move across IEDs still refuses the commit (`MOVE_NOT_FOLLOWED`), and so does a data instance, a data set or a control block moved within the IED, or a device moved to an access point with no connection on the subnetwork of an address it sends.
