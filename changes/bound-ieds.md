### Added

- `query.lifecycle.boundIeds({ scopeRef })` lists the IEDs tied to the LNodes an SSD extract of a Substation, VoltageLevel or Bay writes (the scope's own, the own content of the levels above it, and those of the functions its applications compose), in document order. Each IED carries its classification (`kind`, `basis`, as `classifyIeds`), `assignment: 'full' | 'shared'` (shared when it also serves LNodes outside the scope), and the LNodes on each side, `inside` and `outside`, each split into those it implements (by `lnUuid`, name path, or the `mappedLnUuid` of an unassigned LNode's data) and those specified on it (`LNodeSpecNaming@sIedName`, a name only one IED carries) that another IED or none implements. Types `BoundIed`, `BoundIeds`, `BoundIedsParams`, `BoundLNodes` and `BoundIedAssignment` are exported.
