### Added

- An update shows an element the source holds under another parent as one change, `moved` (`DiffChange` and `DecisionGroup['change']` gain `'moved'`, `DiffNode` gains `movedFrom` and `movedTo`), in every update that pairs by lineage or uuid: an instance's template update and a fork alike. A rename on top of a move rides on the same change; a move into a parent the update adds depends on that parent's group, and the removal of a parent a moved element still sits in depends on the move. Accepted, the element is moved with `tx.move`, keeping its record, before the removals; what names it follows at the commit. Before, such an element was neither reported nor moved. Not yet covered: a process-section primary (a `Function` or an `Application`) that changes level, which is placed by its own pass rather than paired inside a tree. An exhaustive `switch` over `DiffChange` or `DecisionGroup['change']` needs the new case.
